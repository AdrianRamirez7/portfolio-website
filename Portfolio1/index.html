About

I'm a web developer specializing in building modern, responsive websites using content management systems like WordPress and Shopify. My work focuses on creating clean, user-friendly digital experiences using tools such as Elementor while implementing strong on-page SEO, structured content, and optimized layouts. I’ve developed and managed websites for organizations and businesses that need reliable, scalable platforms to communicate their services and engage their audiences. From designing intuitive page structures to ensuring performance across desktop and mobile devices, I focus on delivering websites that are both visually polished and built to perform.

When I’m not coding, you’ll usually find me outdoors surfing, skateboarding, or hiking. I also enjoy exploring emerging technologies like Web XR and experimenting with creative projects such as building small video games with JavaScript. I’m always curious about how new technologies can shape better digital experiences. If you’re looking for someone who can hit the ground running, solve problems, and deliver high-quality results, I’d love the opportunity to work together.
